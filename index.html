02 — ABOUT ME

ABOUT SOUMI

Soumi Mukherjee

Jewelry & Lifestyle Accessories Designer

“Crafting stories through form, detail, and design.”

I am a jewellery design student with a strong inclination toward creating pieces that balance aesthetic elegance with thoughtful functionality.

My design approach is rooted in storytelling — drawing inspiration from cultural elements, historical references, and everyday observations, which I translate into contemporary forms. Through my work, I explore the interplay of structure, detail, and wearability. I enjoy experimenting with form generation, motifs, and mechanisms to create jewellery that is not only visually compelling but also versatile and meaningful to the wearer.

Currently completing my 7th Jury and final year of Bachelor of Design (B.Des) at the prestigious National Institute of Fashion Technology (NIFT), Kolkata. In addition to my design degree, I hold a Diploma in Fine Arts (2024) from Surabharati Sangeet Parishad, grounding my visual storytelling in classical painting and anatomical proportion.

FOUR SIGNATURE COLLECTIONS
COLLECTION 01 • TRANSFORMABLE ADORNMENT
—— VERSAFORMA • 01 MODULAR LUXURY

VersaForma: The Art of Transformable Adornment

Jewellery that evolves in form and function. It reflects the idea that a single piece is not fixed, but fluid — capable of transforming to suit different occasions, styling needs, and identities. Inspired by the ornamental richness of Victorian Jewellery balanced with structured geometric rectangles and triangles.

✦ Convertible Royal Necklace: Features a detachable central drop pendant that connects to an accompanying hair-chain fixture to become a bridal Maang Tikka.
✦ Modular 3-Tier Earrings: Articulated components wearable as dainty top rectangular studs for daytime, or linked with filigree scrolls and pear drops for gala evenings.
✦ Articulated Slave Bracelet (Hathphool): Fluid wrist-to-finger ornament connecting a 14–16 cm top bar bracelet to a cluster-set ring via a center drop chain.
✦ Client Persona: Designed for an elegant, fashion-aware individual (age 22–40) seeking versatile statement pieces with enduring heritage luxury.
18K Yellow Gold Light-Toned Gems Victorian Filigree Modular Clasp 16 cm Drape

COLLECTION 02 • CONTEMPORARY MEN'S JEWELRY
—— RAJDWARA • 02 ARCHITECTURAL SYSTEM

Rajdwara: Sculpting Heritage into Masculine Elegance

A contemporary men’s jewellery collection inspired by the architectural grandeur of Rajasthani gateways (pols), arches (torans), and palace facades. Introduces an interactive modular system of adornment where individual brooches dock into a central royal Kalangi.

✦ Raj Tilak Kalangi (Base Unit): Royal head crest (110 × 40 mm) anchored by an 18 × 12 mm emerald, featuring an integrated rail-slot docking system.
✦ Pravesh Stambh Brooch: Represents palatial gateway strength and स्वागत (welcome) with emerald-cut Colombian emerald and pear drops (70 × 30 mm).
✦ Megh Toran Brooch: Inspired by festive ceremonial torans with five cascading pear-shaped emerald drops and sliding hook attachment.
✦ Surya Pol Brooch: Radiant sun gateway symmetry with floral arabesque detailing, cusped arches, and lock hook system (60 × 35 mm).
18K Yellow Gold Colombian Emeralds Polki Diamonds Rail-Slot System Prong & Pavé

COLLECTION 03 • SACRED GEOMETRY
—— ANTARJYOTI • 03 HIGH JEWELRY BANGLES

अंतर्ज्योति: The Seven Vital Energies

Inspired by the profound journey of self-awakening and the alignment of the seven chakras. Drawing heavily from the Sacred Geometry of the Lotus, radial symmetry, and energy expansion from a singular point of consciousness.

✦ Sahasrara & Padma Bangles: Size 2.6 (60 mm inner diameter) with 8-fold radial repeat crown formations, crafted in 18K yellow gold.
✦ Comprehensive Gemstone Sieve Sheet: Engineered with 220+ round micro-pavé diamonds (GH-VS), 14 oval mixed multi-color gemstones, and hanging natural pearls.
✦ Alta Mudra Hand Modeling: Artistically staged with classical Indian alta-painted mudra hands, expressing devotion, energy, and sacred grace.
✦ Orthographic CAD Drafting: Complete manufacturing blueprints with 2.5 mm metal thickness, motif heights, and setting tolerances.

COLLECTION 04 • ACOUSTIC RESONANCE
—— NAADRAGA • 04 LIFESTYLE ADORNMENT

NaadRaga: The Rhythm of Acoustic Resonance

Explores the intersection of classical Indian acoustic instruments (tanpura, sitar, sarod) and wearable lifestyle accessories. Translating harmonic frequencies, string tension, and resonant wooden chambers into metallic curvature and lifestyle adornment.

✦ Harmonic String Geometry: Visualizing acoustic frequency waves into fluid, rhythmic metal filigree and contour lines.
✦ Instrument Craftsmanship: Drawing from traditional instrument making — warm wood chambers, bone bridges, and brass tension pegs.
✦ Lifestyle Adornments: Expanding beyond fine jewellery into contemporary lifestyle accessories and cultural statement pieces.
