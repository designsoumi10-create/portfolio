Collection 01 • Transformable Adornment
01

VersaForma

The Art of Transformable Adornment

VersaForma represents jewellery that evolves in form and function. It reflects the idea that a single piece is not fixed, but fluid — capable of transforming to suit different occasions, styling needs, and individual identities. Inspired by the ornamental richness of Victorian Jewellery and balanced with structured geometric rectangles and triangles.

Inspiration: Victorian Luxury & Radial Filigree Metals: 18K Yellow Gold Framework Stones: Light-Toned Gemstones & Diamonds Functionality: Modular Detachability

Interactive Modularity Demo: One Piece, Multiple Identities

Collection 02 • Contemporary Men's Jewelry
02

Rajdwara

Sculpting Heritage into Masculine Elegance

Rajdwara is a contemporary men’s jewellery collection inspired by the architectural grandeur of Rajasthani gateways (pols), arches (torans), and palace facades. Expanding beyond static design, the collection introduces an interactive modular system of adornment, where individual brooches attach and detach from a central royal Kalangi.

Palette: Terracotta, Burnt Orange, Mustard, Emerald Green Metal: 18K Yellow Gold with High & Filigree Polish Hero Stones: Octagonal & Pear Colombian Emeralds Mechanism: Rail Slot, Lock Hook, Sliding Hook

The Modular Kalangi System: Architecture Into Interactive Adornment

Inspect the 4 architectural components designed to dock into the central Kalangi base or be worn individually as sherwani brooches.

Complete Raj Tilak Kalangi System: The master royal Rajput head adornment featuring a structured upward architectural flow, massive central emerald, and the integrated rail slot docking system.

01
Rail Slot Base Docking

Kalangi base unit features a precision-machined 5–6 mm rail slot channel accommodating multiple brooch attachments.

02
Dual Utility Brooch Conversion

When removed from the headpiece, each unit functions as a self-standing brooch with hidden needle clasps for sherwanis or bundis.

03
Architectural Symmetry & Emerald Drops

Pear-shaped emerald drops introduce rhythmic movement, evoking the ceremonial torans of Jaipur and Udaipur palaces.

The Seven Vital Energies

Antarjyoti is a collection inspired by the profound journey of self-awakening and the alignment of the seven chakras. In a world of constant motion, this collection serves as a visual anchor representing the "Inner Light". Collection 04 • Acoustic Resonance & Lifestyle Adornment
04

NaadRaga

The Rhythm of Acoustic Resonance

NaadRaga explores the timeless intersection of classical Indian music, stringed acoustic instruments (such as the tanpura, sarod, and sitar), and wearable lifestyle accessories. Translating harmonic frequencies, acoustic string tension, and resonant wooden chambers into fluid metallic curves, rhythmic filigree, and resonant adornment.

Inspiration: Classical Indian String Craft & Ragas Aesthetic: Organic Resonance & Linear Tension Materials: Rose Gold, Warm Brass & Acoustic Wood Accents
